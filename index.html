The Post-Mortem Problem

Traditional HR analytics tools are post-mortem graveyards. They showcase elegant historical turnover bar charts months after high-performing engineers have already resigned. When tools attempt predictive modeling, they output a black-box "Flight Risk: 78%" tag that managers ignore because they don't trust the math and don't know what to do next.
